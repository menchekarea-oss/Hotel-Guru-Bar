# Daily, Previous-Date and Monthly Sales Reports

Sales enter reports only when **Print** is pressed. **Save Bill** only saves the receipt file.

## Open the protected report

1. Press **Locked Sales / Owner only**.
2. Enter the owner password.
3. Press **Unlock Report**.

## Daily report and previous dates

1. Choose **Daily Sales** under Report View.
2. Press the Report Date field to open the calendar.
3. Select any previous date.
4. Use **Previous**, **Today**, and **Next** for quick navigation.
5. Press **Refresh Cloud** while online to download earlier sales from Supabase.

The selected date shows totals, cloud/pending figures, alcoholic drinks, beverages, top items, and individual bills.

## Monthly report

1. Choose **Monthly Sales** under Report View.
2. Select a month using the month calendar.
3. Use **Previous**, **This Month**, and **Next** to move between months.
4. Press **Refresh Cloud** when needed.

Monthly report includes:

- Total bills and sales
- Cloud and pending sales
- Active sales days
- Average sales per active day
- Alcoholic Drinks Sold
- Beverages Sold
- Day-by-day sales breakdown
- Top items for the month
- Up to the latest 100 individual bills in the month

## Previous history

Historical sales are now retained locally for offline report viewing. Supabase remains the cloud source of truth. If an older report is initially empty after updating from an earlier app version, connect to the internet, unlock Sales Report, and press **Refresh Cloud** once. The app retrieves Supabase sales in pages of up to 1,000 records.
